# 4 Pics 1 Anime — Game Show Questionnaire Set

**60 anime · 4 real-world image clues per anime · ordered hardest → easiest**

Clues are objects, scenes, or motifs from the real world (not anime stills). Clue 1 demands lateral thinking; Clue 4 is nearly a giveaway. Rationale included in italics for host reference.

## Hosting notes

- **Difficulty calibration**: Tier 1 is forgiving for casual fans; Tier 3 will separate the hardcore from the rest. Consider scheduling a Tier 3 round as a final.
- **Red herrings**: a few clues (e.g. *bowl-cut child* for Mob Psycho, *blue butterfly* for ERASED vs Casshern Sins) overlap across shows — the 4-clue arc resolves the ambiguity. Watch for contestants locking in too early on clue 1.
